    🤖 How ARTHA works — 60-second tour
    1
    Scan — every trading day at 11:30 & 2:15 (CT) it screens the market with proven, research-backed filters (momentum, strength near 52-week highs, quality trends).
    Junk and lottery-ticket stocks are excluded automatically.
    2
    Research — promising names get deep diligence: live news, filings, earnings, then a council of 3 AI analysts (bull, technician, skeptic) votes with a score out of 100.
    3
    Guardrails — even a “buy” must pass the configured order, daily, position, sector, account-capacity, and broker-review limits. The dashboard shows when a buy is blocked and why.
    4
    Execute — approved orders are placed on the real Robinhood account (the $350 pilot), each with an automatic stop-loss safety net from second one.
    5
    Protect & review — stops trail up as positions win; the sell council re-examines every holding twice a day. Mechanical stops and judgment sells follow the configured execution policy.
    6
    Learn — every night at 9pm it grades its past calls against the market, turns mistakes into lessons, and each Saturday researches the web for ways to improve itself.
